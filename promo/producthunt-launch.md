## Maker's first comment

Hi Product Hunt, I'm David, the maker of Revlur.

I built it because I kept sharing my screen with a whole page of noise around the one thing I wanted people to look at. Revlur lets you drag a box over any part of a page and blurs everything else, so attention goes where you want it.

What you can do with it:
- Select: drag a box on any page
- Zoom: magnify the selected area
- Read: cut distraction from long pages
- Present: keep the audience on the part you're talking about

It's free, needs no account, and collects nothing. The privacy policy is one short page: https://revlur.app/privacy.html

I'd love to hear which sites or workflows you'd use it on, and what's missing. I'm reading every comment today.
