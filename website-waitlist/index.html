How it works.

1

Select

Draw a box around the part of the page you care about.

2

Blur

Everything else fades back, and clicks on the blur do nothing.

3

Zoom

Enlarge it with a pointer dot that shows in screen shares. Copy or save it.

4

Lock-on

Hold the page still while you present. Press Scroll to read on.

What it's made for.

  • Presenting Guide the room to one chart, not the whole dashboard.
  • Teaching Walk through a page one section at a time.
  • Screen sharing Keep side content out of focus while you demo.
  • Reading Long articles and TL;DR posts. Keep only what you're reading in focus.

Light by design

One clickNo menus or pop-ups. Click the icon and start selecting.
Leaves pages aloneRevlur only sits on top of the page. Exit and everything is exactly as it was.
PrivateNo sign-up, no tracking, nothing sent anywhere. The only thing it remembers is your blur level, on your own computer.
Only where you askRevlur works on the page you click it on, and nowhere else. It never watches your other tabs.
