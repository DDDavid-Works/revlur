Privacy policy

Your data stays yours.

Last updated: October 12, 2026

The short version: the Revlur extension collects nothing. This website collects nothing either.

The Revlur extension

Revlur has no accounts, no analytics and no servers. It never sends anything anywhere.

  • What it does on your device. When you click the Revlur icon or use its shortcut, Revlur draws an overlay (the blur, the selection outline and a small toolbar) on the page you are viewing. It runs only on the page you activated it on.
  • Zoom. When you press Zoom, Revlur takes a snapshot of the visible part of that tab and shows part of it enlarged. The snapshot stays in your browser's memory on your computer and is discarded when you close the zoomed view or leave Revlur.
  • Copy and Save. If you press Copy, the image is written to your clipboard. If you press Save, it is downloaded to your computer. Revlur never reads your clipboard and never uploads anything.
  • What it remembers. One setting, your blur level, is saved with Chrome's extension storage on your own device.
  • Permissions. Revlur uses the activeTab, scripting and storage permissions only for the purposes above. It asks for no access to websites in advance and does not run on pages you have not activated it on.

This website

  • What we collect. Nothing. The site has no forms, sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking scripts, and loads no fonts or code from other sites. Like any website, it is delivered through hosting and network providers (Cloudflare and Railway), which process ordinary connection data such as IP addresses in order to serve the pages. We do not use that data.

Children

Revlur and this website are not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the extension ever collects data, this policy will say so before that release.
