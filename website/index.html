Drag to select an area

Four moves. Nothing else.

1

Select

Click the icon and draw a box around the part of the page you care about. That is the only setup.

2

Blur

Everything else fades into the background, so your audience looks where you want. Clicks on the blurred area do nothing, so nothing opens by accident mid-demo.

3

Zoom

Blow up the selected area so the back row can read it. A red pointer dot shows up in screen shares, and you can copy or save the view as an image.

4

Lock-on

Scroll without losing your place. The focus follows the chart, table or paragraph you picked as the page moves.

Made for people who point at screens

  • Presenting Guide the room to one chart, not the whole dashboard.
  • Teaching Walk through a page one section at a time.
  • Screen sharing Keep side content out of focus while you demo.
  • Reading Block distractions around the paragraph you're on.

Light by design

One clickNo menus or pop-ups. Click the icon and start selecting.
Leaves pages aloneRevlur only sits on top of the page. Exit and everything is exactly as it was.
PrivateNo sign-up, no tracking, nothing sent anywhere. The only thing it remembers is your blur level, on your own computer.
Only where you askRevlur works on the page you click it on, and nowhere else. It never watches your other tabs.
