Introducing Revlur

November 7, 2026 · David

Drag a box over any part of a web page and everything else gets blurred, so attention goes where you want it.

Why I built it

I kept sharing my screen with a whole page of noise around the one thing I wanted people to look at. Pointing with the mouse and saying “this bit here” doesn't work when the page is a busy dashboard. Revlur fixes that with one click and one drag.

What you can do with it

  • Select. Click the icon and drag a box around the part of the page you care about. Fine-tune it any time.
  • Zoom. Enlarge the selected area, with a pointer dot that shows up in screen shares. Copy or save the result.
  • Read. Keep only the paragraph you're reading in focus on long pages.
  • Present. Lock the page in place and keep the audience on the part you're talking about.

Free and private

Revlur is free, needs no account and collects nothing. The only thing it remembers is your blur level, on your own computer. The privacy policy is one short page.
